CRYOGENX4 logo options, round 3

Back to the blue, navy and orange family the company already uses. Four options, from the smallest change to the biggest. Each one is shown in full color, on navy, in one color, and at icon sizes.

A. The current logo, cleaned up Smallest change

Same idea and same layout as today: the orange box, three waves carrying heat out, CRYO in orange, GEN in blue, a tall X4 on the right. What changes is the drawing. Flat colors instead of gradients and glow, one stroke weight for the waves, a cleaner typeface, and a small gap where the waves cross the box so it still reads in one color.
