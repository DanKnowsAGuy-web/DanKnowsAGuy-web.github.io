CRYOGENX4 logo concepts, round 2

Bold, confident, industrial. Every concept is drawn in one color first, then tested at icon size, on dark, and on the brand orange. Color is added only where it carries meaning: orange for heat leaving the system.

A. Crossflow X Recommended

The X in the name becomes the mark. One stroke is solid; the other is three tubes, like the coil inside every condenser. The solid stroke passes over the tubes, which is literally what CRYOGENX4 improves: heat moving across the coil. It still reads as a plain X at 16 pixels.

B. Nameplate

Every unit CRYOGENX4 treats has a metal nameplate; that's the first thing a technician reads. The mark is a stamped plate with X4 punched through it and two rivets. Strong on equipment and invoices, a little more literal.
