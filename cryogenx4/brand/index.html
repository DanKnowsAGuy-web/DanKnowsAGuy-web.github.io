The mark alone works as the app icon, LinkedIn avatar and favicon.

The mark: three heat-transfer waves that run from ember orange (heat) into glacier blue (cold), on a deep navy tile. It carries forward the orange box and blue waves in today's logo. The name stays all caps, CRYOGENX4, with X4 in blue.

Voice

We sound like
  • An engineer who has seen your equipment, talking plainly
  • Specific to the building: stores, cases, racks, rooftops
  • Proof first: "we'll show you on one store"
  • Short sentences, real numbers, one clear next step
We avoid
  • "Nanotechnology" and lab language up front
  • Generic HVAC pages that try to talk to everyone
  • Claims without a source or a qualifier
  • Menus and links that pull people off the story
