CRYOGENX4

Brand direction, draft 2. Bold, confident, industrial: built like the equipment it treats, and orange because orange is the heat it moves out.

Logo

The recommended mark is the Crossflow X: the X in the name, drawn as a solid bar passing over three coil tubes. One color first; orange marks the bar (heat leaving) when color is available.

Two families on a clear contrast: tall industrial signage for headlines, and a typeface built for low-vision readers for everything people need to read.

Voice

Sound like

  • A field engineer who has been on the roof
  • The store's language: cases, racks, margin, sales
  • Proof first: "we'll show you on one store"
  • Real numbers, each with a source or qualifier

Never sound like

  • "Nanotechnology" or lab hype up front
  • A generic HVAC contractor
  • A tech startup or a green eco brand
  • A miracle gadget with no qualifier

Recurring motifs

Three devices borrowed from the equipment world replace the usual stat strips and card grids.

Spec plate

How it works, laid out like a unit's nameplate: label, value, rivets.

Ledger

What a store gets back, as line items with leaders, like an invoice.
