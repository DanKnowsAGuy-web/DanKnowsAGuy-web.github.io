Voice

We sound like

  • An engineer who has seen your equipment, talking plainly
  • The store's own language: cases, racks, margin, sales
  • Proof first: "we'll show you on one store"
  • Real numbers, each with a source or a qualifier

We avoid

  • "Nanotechnology" and lab language up front
  • Sounding like a generic HVAC contractor
  • Tech-startup or green eco language
  • Claims without a source or a qualifier
