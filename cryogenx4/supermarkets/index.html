It costs more to run every year.

Refrigeration loses ground with age. Compressor oil slowly coats the inside of the coils and lines, so heat moves out more slowly. The compressors run longer to hold the same temperature, and the bill climbs even when nothing else changes.

On grocery margins, that matters more than in almost any other business.

ENERGY STAR's estimate for supermarkets, where net profit runs about 1 to 2 percent of sales.

How CRYOGENX4 works

Install
A certified technician adds it to the refrigerant circuit while the system runs. No modifications, no shutdown, no product moved.
What it does
Works on the internal metal surfaces to improve heat transfer at the condenser, evaporator and compressor.
Result
Cases and rooms reach temperature faster, so compressors run less and draw less power.
Equipment
1 to 1,600 tons: reach-ins, walk-in coolers and freezers, rack systems and rooftop units.
