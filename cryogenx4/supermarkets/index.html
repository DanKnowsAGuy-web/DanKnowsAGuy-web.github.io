Why this matters in grocery

Your biggest electric load is losing ground every year.

In a supermarket, refrigeration runs around the clock and uses more electricity than anything else in the building. As it ages, it quietly costs more to do the same job.

40–60%

Refrigeration leads the bill

Cases, walk-ins and racks use 40 to 60 percent of a typical supermarket's electricity.

Every year

Efficiency slips with age

Over time, compressor oil coats the inside of coils and lines. Heat moves out more slowly, so compressors run longer to hold the same temperature.

$1 = $59

Savings act like sales

On grocery's thin margins, ENERGY STAR estimates every dollar saved on energy is worth about $59 in new sales.

How CRYOGENX4 works

One treatment. The equipment you already own works like it should.

Added to the refrigerant circuit

A certified technician adds CRYOGENX4 while the system runs. No modifications, no shutdown, no product moved.

Restores heat transfer

It works on the internal metal surfaces and improves heat transfer at the condenser, evaporator and compressor.

Compressors run less

Cases and rooms reach temperature faster, so compressors cycle less and draw less power for the same cold.

Treats equipment from 1 to 1,600 tons: reach-ins, walk-in coolers and freezers, rack systems and rooftop units. One time, for the remaining life of the equipment.
